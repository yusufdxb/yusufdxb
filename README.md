## What I build

### 01 / Reliable autonomy

Robots have to notice failure, hold themselves safe, recover, and leave behind evidence you can reconstruct afterwards.

HELIX · policy-health-monitor · BlackBoxRS

### 02 / Sim-to-real systems

A policy is only worth training if the thing that worked in simulation survives deployment intact.

GO2-Phoenix · IVF · PhysX/Newton benchmark

### 03 / Embodied intelligence

Perception, language and human intent have to turn into physical robot behaviour.

Come Here · GO2 Seeing-Eye Dog · semantic navigation · open-vocabulary mapping

## Selected systems

### HELIX

Self-healing runtime for ROS 2 robots.

**8 live robot sessions · 30 anomalies · 14 recovery actions**

`ROS 2 · C++`

[Code](https://github.com/yusufdxb/helix) · [Demo](https://youtu.be/PbKXB91-NSY)

### GO2-Phoenix

Sim-to-real locomotion with parity-gated deployment and fail-closed safety.

**32/32 simulated stand successes · ONNX deploy parity enforced**

`Isaac Lab · PPO · ONNX · ROS 2`

[Code](https://github.com/yusufdxb/go2-phoenix) · [Evidence](https://github.com/yusufdxb/go2-phoenix/blob/main/EVIDENCE.md) · [Demo](https://youtu.be/Nu0oWyJJbEM)

### supercombo-blindspot

Failure-awareness study of openpilot's driving model under distribution shift.

**1159-frame parity-controlled reproduction · shifted inputs expose silent internal collapse**

`ONNX Runtime · CARLA · OOD detection`

[Code](https://github.com/yusufdxb/supercombo-blindspot) · [Demo](https://youtu.be/tnM18XGbNMY)

### Come Here

Voice-triggered caller localisation and approach on a quadruped.

**Hear, localise and rotate validated end to end on hardware**

`ROS 2 · mic array · Whisper`

[Code](https://github.com/yusufdxb/come-here)
